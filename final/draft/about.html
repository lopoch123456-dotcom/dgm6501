About PLUMI

Where luxury craftsmanship meets pet accessories.

Our Story

PLUMI was born from a simple belief: your pet deserves the same elegance you do. Founded in 2024, we set out to bring fine-jewelry craftsmanship into the world of pet accessories — breaking away from the generic and the ordinary.

Every charm, collar tag, and accessory is designed with the same attention to detail you'd find in a high-end jeweler's studio. We work with carefully sourced materials and partner with artisan makers who share our obsession with quality.

Our Philosophy

We don't make pet products. We make wearable art for the animals who have earned a place in your life. PLUMI pieces are built to last, made to be noticed, and designed to feel as good as they look.

From the Star Charm Set to our upcoming seasonal drops, every PLUMI piece carries a story — one of craft, intention, and deep respect for the bond between you and your pet.

2026 Collection

Our 2026 lineup is our boldest yet. Drawing inspiration from celestial motifs and architectural gold, this collection reimagines what a pet accessory can be. Each piece is limited edition and crafted in small batches to ensure uncompromising quality.
